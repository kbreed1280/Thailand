WanderHub Privacy Policy

Last updated October 10, 2026

WanderHub is a trip planner. We built it so your trip stays yours. We don't run ads, we don't track you across apps or websites, and we don't sell data.

Where your data lives

Your trips, itinerary, saved spots, lists, notes, photos, expenses and documents are stored on your iPhone and in your own iCloud account (Apple's CloudKit private database). We, the developer, cannot see them. If you share a trip with someone, Apple's iCloud sharing makes it visible to the people you invite.

Links and screenshots you import

When you share a TikTok, Instagram, YouTube, Google Maps or web link to WanderHub, the app reads that post's public caption, description and location tag on your iPhone, and searches Apple Maps to place the spots on your map. Screenshots are read on your device with Apple's text recognition. Videos and images are not copied or uploaded anywhere.

Location

With your permission, WanderHub uses your location while the app is open to show where you are, distances to your spots, walking directions and nearby places. Your location is not stored on our servers or shared.

Community sharing (optional, off by default)

If you turn on Share my spots with the community in Profile, spots you confirm are shared anonymously with other WanderHub users through Apple's CloudKit public database: the place name and location, the inside scoop (for example, what to order), and the link to the original public post. Your notes, photos, trips and lists are never shared. Your @username is shown only if you choose to. Turning the setting off deletes what you shared. You can report or block community content from the ⋯ menu on any tip. A report stores the place, the reason and an anonymous app ID so we can review it; reported content is reviewed within 24 hours.

Services we use

  • Apple (iCloud/CloudKit, Maps, WeatherKit, StoreKit for subscriptions), under Apple's privacy policy.
  • Open-Meteo for weather if Apple Weather is unavailable. Only a location is sent.
  • Wikipedia for photos of landmarks. Only a place's coordinates are sent.
  • Public exchange-rate and flight-status services, which receive only a currency or flight number.

Subscriptions

Purchases are handled entirely by Apple. We never see your payment details.

Children

WanderHub is not directed at children under 13.

Deleting your data

Delete a trip in the app, or delete the app and remove WanderHub's data in Settings → [your name] → iCloud → Manage Storage. Community tips are removed when you turn community sharing off.
