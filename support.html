Common questions

How do I save a spot from TikTok or Instagram?
Tap Share on the post, then WanderHub. It appears in Spots → Drafts for you to confirm.

A post didn't find the place.
Open Drafts and tap Research. WanderHub reads the post's location tag and on-screen text and searches again.

How do I cancel my subscription?
Settings → [your name] → Subscriptions → WanderHub Pro → Cancel Subscription.

How do I restore a purchase?
Profile → WanderHub Pro → Restore purchases.
